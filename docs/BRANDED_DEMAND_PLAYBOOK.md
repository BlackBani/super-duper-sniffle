# Branded-demand playbook

Repository-ready assets are the Pauch About page, brand directory, terminology guide, delivery/order pages, stable identity/schema, and optimized imagery. Off-site execution remains owner-controlled.

Use `Pauch` consistently on verified Telegram and other official profiles; use the same logo, short category descriptor, canonical site link, and current ordering/delivery facts. Do not create duplicate profiles, artificial searches, fake reviews, fake scarcity, paid-link networks, youth-oriented promotion, or unsupported manufacturer/distributor claims.

Potential owner-approved touchpoints: order confirmation, courier/support message, packaging insert or receipt, a stable QR link, post-delivery support, and factual adult-oriented strength/delivery/brand resources. Any outreach must use approved business facts and image rights. Track exact Pauch mentions, unlinked mentions, impersonation, incorrect order information, branded query share, direct/returning traffic, and assisted orders monthly.
